**Future work to bear in mind please**

The link in bio page (Page 1\) — keep on Kajabi for now.

Do not rebuild your link-in-bio hub on your own website. Kajabi is actually a strong place for this because:

	•	It updates fast

	•	It handles all the traffic

	•	It integrates directly with your buyer accounts

	•	Redesigning it on your website is a lot of work for very little SEO benefit (nobody Googles for a link-in-bio page)

The one thing to change: give this Kajabi hub a branded URL. So instead of the ugly mykajabi.com link, use tobiyusuf.com/start or tobiyusuf.com/hello or tobiyusuf.com/links — which redirects to the Kajabi hub. Your Instagram bio then reads tobiyusuf.com/start. Branded, clean, and Kajabi still does the work behind the scenes.

The individual sales pages (Page 2\) — this is where you build on your own website.

The Understanding Series and Closer are your two flagship products. These deserve dedicated pages on your own website because:

	•	They are what Google will rank

	•	They are what press and podcast hosts will link to

	•	They are what buyers will remember and share

	•	They carry your brand authority forward

So the plan is:

	•	tobiyusuf.com/understanding-series — a landing page on your own website. Introduces the series, includes testimonials as they come in, has a big button that says Get The Series → sends people to the Kajabi checkout.

	•	tobiyusuf.com/closer — same setup for Closer.

	•	tobiyusuf.com/reflection-call — same for the Marriage Reflection Call when you are ready.

Each of these is on your website. Each of them hands off to Kajabi only at the moment of purchase.

The flow:

Instagram bio → tobiyusuf.com/start (Kajabi hub, but branded URL)

Someone clicks “The Understanding Series” on that hub → tobiyusuf.com/understanding-series (your website landing page)

They read, they scroll, they click “Get The Series” → Kajabi checkout page

They buy → Kajabi handles delivery, email sequence, everything

Why this is the strongest setup:

	•	Your website builds SEO on the two most important pages

	•	Kajabi handles the transactional work (checkout, delivery, emails) — because it is genuinely good at it

	•	The user experience feels seamless — they never notice the handoff

	•	Your brand grows on your own domain
